Privacy Policy

Nested: Task Strategy • Studio X Anywhere
Effective Date: September 8, 2026

✓ Zero Data Collection Verified

1. Overview

Nested: Task Strategy is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. The application operates entirely offline-first. We believe your productivity, projects, workflows, and task hierarchies belong solely to you.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user accounts.
  • No Task Content: All tasks, subtasks, descriptions, completion states, and weights remain stored locally on your device.
  • No Location Data: The application does not request, access, or track device GPS or geographic location.
  • No Financial or Payment Data: No payment details or purchase histories are collected through the application.

3. Data Storage & Security

All application data is stored locally in your device's private storage (such as Android SharedPreferences or Web LocalStorage). Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it using the application's manual backup and export features.

4. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Nested: Task Strategy does not integrate advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics engines, or third-party behavioral profiling tools.

5. Permissions Requested

The application requires minimal device permissions. It does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or system accounts.

6. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy:

  • Data Collected: None.
  • Data Shared: None.
  • Encryption in Transit: Not applicable (no network transmission occurs).
  • Account Deletion: Not applicable (no account creation required). You may permanently erase all app data by clearing the application storage in device settings or deleting the app.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Studio X Anywhere through our official repository:

https://github.com/studioxanywhere-hub/Nested-TaskStrategy
